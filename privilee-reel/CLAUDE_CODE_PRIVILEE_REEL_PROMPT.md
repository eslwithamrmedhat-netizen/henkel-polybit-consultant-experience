## 6. Colour grading and cinematic finish

My established preference is natural, believable colour with a soft golden-hour touch. I dislike artificial backgrounds, exaggerated saturation, over-sharpening, and visibly processed skin.

Create a coherent sequence while respecting the light in each scene.

### Pass 1: correct and normalize

Inspect the source metadata. Correct orientation, exposure, white balance, and obvious colour casts. Distinguish SDR, HDR/HLG, and any confirmed Log footage; do not apply a Log conversion to standard footage. Use the appropriate transform or tone mapping where needed. Simply adding SDR metadata does not convert an HDR image.

### Pass 2: create the look

| Scene | Intended treatment |
|---|---|
| Work / city | Clean neutrals, restrained saturation, believable daylight, and controlled contrast. |
| Indoor gym | Neutral skin, corrected green/magenta lighting casts, slightly cooler shadows if appropriate, and visible detail in dark clothing and equipment. |
| Pools / beach | Soft warm highlights, natural skin, controlled blue/aqua water, and detail in white clothing, clouds, and reflections. |
| Golden-hour portrait | Gentle warmth consistent with the source light; retained texture and believable shadow colour. |

Avoid an aggressive teal-and-orange look, orange skin, neon water, crushed blacks, excessive haze, fake lens flares, and broad skin smoothing. Use subtle grain only if it improves the full-resolution output; leave it off if it makes phone footage look noisy or damages compression.

### Pass 3: match the sequence

Compare adjacent shots at edit speed. Match apparent exposure, saturation, skin colour, and contrast so viewers do not see abrupt colour jumps. Keep any preset/LUT strength adjustable per clip. Do not use fixed grading values across differently lit photos and videos.

Export a small before/after contact sheet from representative gym, work, outdoor, and skin-tone shots. Inspect it before final rendering.

### Delivery specification

These are production choices for this project, not a claim that every value is a platform minimum:

- MP4 container, H.264 video, yuv420p, AAC audio where audio is included.
