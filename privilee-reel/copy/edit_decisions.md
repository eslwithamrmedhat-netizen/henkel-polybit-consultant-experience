Built from the shared album: 118 items, which became 160 files once Live Photos were split into stills plus short clips. 29 sources were used. All of them were audited and graded (see `config/asset_manifest.json`, `qa/contact_sheets/` and `qa/grade/`).

## The idea on screen

**The same mirror selfie, in a different gym every time.** About 20 photos showed this habit, and six of them play as a quick 0.45-second montage. Together with a **pull-up match cut**, where the same movement continues from a teal gym into a neon gym, it shows "Different cities. Same routine." without needing to explain it.

## Deliberate exclusions

- Group photos and the Muay Thai class, because they contain other identifiable people.
- UFC fight posters (copyrighted artwork).
- Selfies with WHOOP stat overlays (third-party branding).
- The padel clip (sponsor banners and a QR code).
- The food photo.
- The White Friday artwork, which is a reference and not personal footage. It's filed in `brand/`.

## Honesty rules applied

- **No location labels on screen.** Only one clip (IMG_1301, the open-air gym) has GPS, and it places it in Dubai. Nothing is labelled by filename.
- The possible Doha skyline (IMG_4530) has no location data, so it isn't used as Qatar evidence.
- The Privilee app icon in the album is only 305 px, so Privilee is mentioned as restrained text instead of an upscaled logo.
- **All original clip audio is muted.** Several clips are saved app edits that may contain music, and nothing has been listened to.

## Why these are review cuts

The ElevenLabs key was rejected and no cleared music was supplied. So every narrated deliverable is rendered as a **text-led review cut** (`*_textled_review.mp4`): short on-screen lines tell the story, and the audio track is silent. Once narration is integrated, the lines switch off automatically and timed captions take over.
