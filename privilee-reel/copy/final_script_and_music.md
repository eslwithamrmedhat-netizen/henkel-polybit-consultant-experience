## Background music

### What to look for
- **Style:** warm melodic / organic house. It should be bright but not aggressive, with soft synths and a clean kick.
- **Tempo:** about **112 BPM**. At that tempo a half-beat is ≈ 0.27 s, which matches the 0.28 s selfie/freeze cuts, so they land on the rhythm.
- **Shape across the film:**
  - 0–10 s: intro, filtered or soft kick, room for the hook line
  - ~10 s: full beat drops as the gyms start
  - 18.7–24.6 s: peak energy for the fast montage
  - 24.6 s: punchy section for the fight beat
  - 32.1 s: lift for the countries beat
  - ~35.3 s: breakdown for the pools
  - 45.5 s: build back for the recap burst
  - 49.3 s: resolve and hold for the offer card
- **Mix:** keep music under the voice. In-app, set the original sound (voice) at ~100% and the music at ~25–35%.

### Track ideas (check that each is in the app's library on posting day)
- **Adam Port, Stryv, Keinemusik — "Move"**: groovy, very current, great under fast cuts.
- **Black Coffee, David Guetta, Delilah Montagu — "Drive"**: sunny, polished, Dubai-weekend feel.
- **Ben Böhmer — "Beyond Beliefs"**: melodic, cinematic, elegant for the pools and the offer card.
- **Bob Moses — "Tearing Me Up"**: moody-cool, works if you want it less "summer".
- **RÜFÜS DU SOL — "Innerbloom"**: atmospheric; use a later section, since its intro is long.

**Searches in Instagram / TikTok libraries:** "organic house", "melodic house", "deep house summer", "Dubai sunset", "afro house chill".

### Licensing (important)
- Add music **inside each app** from that app's own library. Don't download a track from one app and re-upload it to the other.
- If the post uses Instagram's **Paid partnership** label or TikTok's **commercial content disclosure**, the library may only offer cleared commercial tracks. On TikTok, pick from the **Commercial Music Library**. Popular charting songs may not be available then.
- **One track baked into the file for both apps** (and any future brand reuse) needs a licence covering both platforms and commercial use. The usual sources are a paid Epidemic Sound or Artlist plan (search "organic house 112 bpm"). Check the plan's terms first; nothing has been bought.
